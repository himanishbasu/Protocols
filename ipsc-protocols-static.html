iPSC Thawing Protocol

Thaw, plate, and recover cryopreserved iPSCs.

1. Growth Media

Supplemented StemFlex media, no ROCKi. Make this first — it's the base media used in the thawing/plating recipe below, and for regular feeding once cells have recovered from thawing or passaging.

Growth Media

Supplemented StemFlex — stable at 4°C for 1 month

Total volume needed: 40 mL

StemFlex media (Thermo #A3349401)36 mL
StemFlex media Supplement4 mL

2. Make thawing media

Combine supplemented StemFlex media with ROCKi (Y-27632) at a 1:1000 ratio. This is the thawing/plating media used to resuspend the cell pellet and to feed the dish once cells are plated.

Thawing / Plating Media

Supplemented StemFlex + ROCKi (1:1000)

Media volume needed: 11 mL

Supplemented StemFlex media11 mL
ROCKi (Y-27632, 1:1000)11 µL

3. Prepare plates

Coat one CellBind 60 mm dish with Synthemax coating solution.

  • Add 6–8 mL of diluted Synthemax to the dish and incubate for 2 hours at room temperature in the hood.

Synthemax Coating Solution

1 mL Synthemax + 20 mL water — stable at 4°C for 2 months

Total volume needed: 21 mL

Synthemax stock1 mL
ddH2O20 mL

4. Warm up thawing media to room temperature

5. Thaw cells

If thawing from unknown source, it is good to thaw in 35mm wells or 1 well of a 6 well plate to avoid excessive dilution
  • Retrieve the cryovial of cryopreserved iPSCs from liquid nitrogen storage.
  • Thaw quickly in a 37°C bath (~2 minutes).
  • Wipe the vial with 70% ethanol and bring it into the hood.
  • Transfer the thawed cells into a 15 mL tube containing 3 mL DPBS.
  • Centrifuge at 300 × g for 5 minutes.
  • Aspirate the supernatant as much as possible, leaving just enough to keep the pellet submerged.
  • Resuspend the pellet in 1 mL thawing media, triturating gently 2–3 times.
Do not dissociate to single cells — keep clumps of roughly 10–100 cells.
  • Aspirate the Synthemax coating solution completely from the dish, without scratching the plate surface.
  • Add 6 mL of the warmed thawing/plating media to the dish.
  • Add the 1 mL cell suspension to the dish.
  • Gently rock the plate to distribute the cells evenly.
  • After 24 hours, replace the media with growth media (supplemented StemFlex media, without ROCKi).

6. Maintenance

Completely change media every 1–2 days with supplemented StemFlex.

iPSC Passaging

1. Do it when the cells reach ~85% confluency.

2. Prepare fresh plate with Synthemax coating

Coat one CellBind 60 mm dish with Synthemax coating solution.

  • Add 6–8 mL of diluted Synthemax to the dish and incubate for 2 hours at room temperature in the hood.

Synthemax Coating Solution

1 mL Synthemax + 20 mL water — stable at 4°C for 2 months

Total volume needed: 21 mL

Synthemax stock1 mL
ddH2O20 mL

3. Prepare Accutase

Dilute stock Accutase 1:3 in PBS.

Accutase Dilution

1:3 final dilution in PBS — stable at 4°C for 2 months

Total volume needed: 15 mL

Accutase stock5 mL
PBS10 mL

4. Passage cells

Wash cells with 1× PBS:

  • Remove all media from the well quickly with suction.
  • Add 5 mL of PBS gently to the side.
  • Remove all PBS from the well with suction.

Dissociate with Accutase:

  • Add 1 mL Accutase into the well (gently to the side).
  • Remove most of it by suction, leaving only a thin film on the plate.
  • Incubate in the hood for 1 minute.
  • Add 1 mL PBS very gently to the side.
Cells are very loosely attached at this point — handle gently.
  • Aspirate the Accutase + PBS with suction.
Keep the plate flat while aspirating the Accutase + PBS.
  • Add 5 mL of StemFlex media.
  • Scrape cells very minimally (1–2 scrapes) to release them from the bottom of the well.
  • Triturate very minimally (1–2 times) with a 1 mL pipette to break up large clumps gently.
  • Clumps of 50–100 cells are ideal.

Transfer cells (~1.5 mL cell suspension, 1:3 passage) dropwise onto the new coated plate with fresh media.

iPSC Freezing

1. Pre-cool Mr. Frostie to 4°C (takes 1 hour).

2. Make freezing media

KSR supplement with 10% DMSO. Pre-cool to 4°C.

Freezing Media

KSR supplement with 10% DMSO — pre-cool to 4°C

Total volume needed: 11.1 mL

KSR supplement10 mL
DMSO1.1 mL

If in doubt, this media can be filter sterilized through a 0.22 µm filter.

3. Pre-label cryotubes, loosen caps, and keep ready in the hood (personnel, cell type, passage, date).

4. Freeze cells at 70–80% confluency, one tube per well.

5. Wash cells with 1× PBS (as in passaging).

6. Dissociate with Accutase (1:3 in PBS)

  1. Add 1 mL Accutase into the well.
  2. Remove immediately.
  3. Incubate in the hood for 1 minute.
  4. Add 1 mL PBS.
  5. Aspirate the Accutase + PBS.
  6. Add 1 mL of pre-cooled freezing media (KSR supplement with 10% DMSO).
  7. Scrape cells to release them from the bottom of the well.
  8. Triturate well with a 1 mL pipette to break up clumps gently.
  • Just a few passes are enough to break large clumps into smaller ones — don't make single cells; clumps should have roughly 100–1000 cells each.

7. Transfer to cryotubes.

8. Put cryotubes in Mr. Frostie.

IMPORTANT: DMSO is toxic to the cells. Perform Steps 4f to 6 as fast as possible.

9. Put Mr. Frostie in -80°C overnight.

10. Transfer the cryotubes to liquid nitrogen (LN2).
