Laminin-521 Coating Protocol

Coat culture vessels with Laminin-521 at 5 µg/mL (1:20 from the 100 µg/mL stock).

1. Materials

  • Laminin-521 #200-0117 (100 µg/mL stock)
  • DMEM/F-12, or DPBS with calcium and magnesium
  • Sterile 1.5 mL tubes for aliquots
Calcium and magnesium are required in the diluent. Calcium is needed for laminin to assemble correctly on the surface, and magnesium is needed for the integrin binding site to work. DMEM/F-12 already contains both (1.05 mM Ca, 0.71 mM Mg), so it can be used directly. Plain PBS without calcium and magnesium cannot.

2. Aliquot the stock (first thaw only)

  • Thaw the vial slowly at 2–8°C.
Do not thaw at room temperature or in a 37°C water bath.
  • Mix by gently inverting the tube. Do not vortex.
  • Aliquot 500 µL per tube. A 500 µg vial (5 mL at 100 µg/mL) gives ~10 aliquots.
  • Store the aliquots at −80°C. Each aliquot is thawed once only.
  • A thawed aliquot can be kept at 2–8°C for up to 3 months. Do not refreeze it.

3. Make working solution

  • Thaw one 500 µL aliquot by resuspending in 500 µL cold DMEM/F-12.
Thaw the aliquot on ice and keep the DMEM/F-12 cold. A sudden temperature change causes Laminin-521 to coagulate.
  • Mix very gently.
  • Add 9 mL of DMEM/F-12 or DPBS with Ca²⁺/Mg²⁺ to make 10 mL total.
  • Final dilution is 1:20.

4. Coat vessels

Add the working solution to the vessel:

6 cm dish2 mL
10 cm dish5 mL
T-75 flask7 mL
6-well plate1 mL / well
12-well plate0.5 mL / well
24-well plate200 µL / well
  • Tilt the vessel to spread the solution over the entire surface.
  • Incubate at 37°C for 2 hours, then proceed to seeding.

If not using immediately

  • Seal with parafilm and incubate at 4°C overnight instead.
  • Coated vessels can be held at 4°C for up to 4 weeks.
  • If held longer than 1–2 weeks, check for evaporation and top up with DPBS (Ca²⁺/Mg²⁺) as needed.

5. Before seeding

  • Aspirate the coating solution immediately before seeding. No need to wash.
Aspirate only immediately before seeding so the surface does not dry out.
